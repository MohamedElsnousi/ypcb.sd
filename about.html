OUR JOURNEY

Legacy, Mission & Sectors

Origins & Formation

The idea of establishing the organization came after the convergence of viewpoints and ideas of a bright group of young people came together, and the beginning was in the state of South Darfur, the city of Nyala. This group began its humanitarian work by calling for the Dankoj campaign, which is a campaign to collect clothing and distribute it to the displaced people in the city of Buram after the outbreak of civil war among the tribes in that region, and that was in the year 2016. The group continued its humanitarian work and built its ability to succeed in benefiting from the energies of youth in bridging and closing the gap between the warring blocs within local communities. In the year 2022, the organization saw the light and it was officially registered with the Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC) in South Darfur State, in this year 2023 a new office was opened after the outbreak of 15 April war in Sennar State.

Our Mission

Empowering young people, working to improve the quality of education, and creating alternative education opportunities for young men and women of school age who have not entered the education field for many reasons is the organization’s primary mission. The organization also seeks to build the capabilities of national organizations and other national institutions through its young cadres and conduct studies that determine the organization’s capabilities (Organizational Capacity Index, OCI) to determine the required intervention. The scope of the organization's mission expands to include striving to improve dire humanitarian conditions in times of disasters and harnessing the maximum energies of youth to build strong national economies.

Our Vision

The organization's management aspires to the existence of healthy, and peaceful societies governed by a democracy of opinion, where tolerance and acceptance of others prevail, and the rule of law prevails. The organization also hopes that young people will have opportunities for good education and that young people will participate in building peace effectively and building the economy by establishing strong national institutions. and has the ability to benefit from all the resources present in the country and harness these resources to serve the citizens and achieve a degree of well-being to ensure a decent and secure life for all components of society.
